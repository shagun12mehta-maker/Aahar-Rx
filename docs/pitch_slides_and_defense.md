## Podium Q&A Defense (Preparing for Tough Jury Questions)

### Q1: "Why create a rule engine when an LLM like ChatGPT or Gemini can just answer food-drug interactions?"
**Your Winning Answer:**
> *"Generative AI can hallucinate dosages, fail on regional Indian dietary nuances, and give inconsistent medical advice across multiple queries. In medical applications, determinism is non-negotiable. At Aahar-Rx, our AI Vision (Member 1) only identifies the salt and food; the interaction engine runs on **100% deterministic, doctor-verified rules grounded in ICMR-NIN and FDA data**. Zero hallucinations, full auditability."*

### Q2: "Isn't it easier to tell the patient to simply stop drinking tea or stop eating curd?"
**Your Winning Answer:**
> *"In behavioral healthcare, restrictive prohibitions cause immediate non-adherence. You cannot ask an Indian patient to abandon morning chai or daily dahi. Aahar-Rx's core innovation is the **Safe Time-Gap Model and 'Ghar-Ki-Rasoi' Kitchen Swaps**. For instance, with iron tablets, we don't ban tea forever; we introduce a 2-hour window and offer Nimbu Pani or Amla juice—whose Vitamin C actively converts non-heme $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ into absorbable $\text{Fe}^{2+}$. We preserve cultural diets while maximizing pharmacological efficacy."*

### Q3: "How does Aahar-Rx comply with Medical Device and Safety Regulations?"
**Your Winning Answer:**
> *"Aahar-Rx strictly adheres to clinical safety boundaries: we **never alter, titrate, or substitute prescribed drugs or dosages**. Our recommendations are strictly limited to dietary timing intervals and nutrient swaps. Every rule in `verified_rules.json` contains full provenance—citing specific FDA monograph IDs, ICMR-NIN table numbers, and clinical sign-off records."*
