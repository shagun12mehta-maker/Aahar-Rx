# Aahar-Rx: "Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" (घर की रसोई) Kitchen-Swap Library
**Compiled by:** Member 2 (Clinical Rules & Data)  
**Philosophy:** In Indian households, telling a patient *"Stop eating this food forever"* leads to immediate diet non-compliance. Our clinical philosophy is **Substitution, Timing, and Culinary Neutralization**—preserving the joy of Indian food while protecting drug bioavailability.

---

## 1. Thyroid Hormone (Levothyroxine)
*Challenge: Empty stomach absorption is blocked by milk tea, curd, and soy.*

| Meal Scenario | High-Conflict Food | "Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" Swap Option 1 | "Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" Swap Option 2 | Culinary & Biochemical Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Early Morning 7:00 AM** | Masala Chai with whole Buffalo Milk | **Kasaath / Warm Lemon Water with Jeera** | **Black Tulsi & Ginger Tea (No Milk)** | Delay milky chai by at least 60 minutes. Warm water and lemon prime gastric acid ($pH < 2.5$) for optimal Levothyroxine tablet dissolution. |
| **Breakfast / Lunch** | Soya Chunks Curry / Soya Vadi | **Paneer Bhurji (lunch only, 4 hrs post-pill)** | **Besan Chilla with Mint Chutney** | Soy contains genistein and daidzein (isoflavones) which inhibit thyroid peroxidase and compete for mucosal carriers. |
| **Evening Snack** | Roasted Walnuts / Soya Khakhra | **Roasted Makhana (Foxnuts) with turmeric & pinch of salt** | **Poha with roasted peanuts & lemon** | Makhana offers crispness and dietary fiber without interfering with thyroid hormone homeostasis. |

## 2. Iron Supplements (Ferrous Ascorbate / Sulphate)
*Challenge: Tannins in tea and oxalates in greens precipitate iron into unabsorbable rocks.*

| Meal Scenario | High-Conflict Food | "Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" Swap Option 1 | "Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" Swap Option 2 | Culinary & Biochemical Mechanism |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Post-Meal Fatigue Drink** | Kadak Masala Chai or South Indian Filter Coffee | **Freshly Squeezed Nimbu Pani (Lemonade with pinch of roasted jeera)** | **Amla Juice or Guava Slices (Amrood)** | Ascorbic Acid (Vitamin C) acts as an electron donor, reducing ferric ($\text{Fe}^{3+}$) to soluble ferrous ($\text{Fe}^{2+}$), boosting enterocyte absorption up to 300%. |
| **Lunch Curry** | Palak Paneer | **Palak Aloo or Palak Corn + 1/2 Lemon juice squeeze** | **Methi Malai Matar replaced by Matar Mushroom** | Paneer introduces high $\text{Ca}^{2+}$ (480mg/100g) which competes directly for the divalent metal transporter 1 (DMT-1). Squeezing lemon breaks oxalate bonds. |
| **Dinner Bread** | Coarse Wheat Bran Roti (Heavy Choker) | **Jowar (Sorghum) Roti or Bajra Roti** | **Missi Roti (Besan + Atta)** | Wheat bran is dense in phytic acid (840mg/100g). Traditional fermentation or swapping to millets reduces phytate load. |
