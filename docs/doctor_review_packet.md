## 1. Project Background & Rationale
In India, polypharmacy is common among chronic disease patients (thyroid disorders, hypertension, diabetes, anemia). Crucially, typical Indian dietary staples—such as morning masala milk tea (chai), curd (dahi), palak (spinach), sarson (mustard greens), and sendha namak—are dense in bioactive cations, phytates, tannins, and Vitamin K.

When taken concurrently with modern pharmaceuticals, these traditional foods cause severe **pharmacokinetic impairment (reduced $C_{\max}$, delayed $T_{\max}$, dropped bioavailability)** or **pharmacodynamic antagonism**.

Aahar-Rx bridges this gap by offering:
1. **Scientific Evidence-Backed Safe Time Gaps** (instead of stopping foods or medications).
2. **"Ghar-Ki-Rasoi" Practical Swaps**: Culturally acceptable dietary alternatives that prevent therapeutic failure without altering physician-prescribed dosages.
