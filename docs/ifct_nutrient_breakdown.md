## 2. Polyphenols, Tannins & Phytates
*Relevant Drug Classes: Iron Supplements / Hematinics (RULE-IRO-002), Levothyroxine (RULE-THY-001)*

| Food Item / Beverage | IFCT Section | Total Polyphenols / Tannins | Phytic Acid / Phytates | Mechanism on DMT-1 Iron Transporters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Masala Chai (Brewed)** | Beverages (Sec 3) | **105 mg GAE / 100ml** | Trace | Tannins form insoluble iron-tannate precipitates; drops iron uptake by >60%. |
| **Filter Coffee** | Beverages (Sec 3) | **120 mg GAE / 100ml** | Trace | Chlorogenic acids reduce non-heme iron absorption by 40–50%. |
| **Palak (Spinach)** | Table A (Greens) | **92 mg / 100g** | **658 mg Oxalates** | High oxalate-to-iron ratio renders inherent plant iron unabsorbable and chelates supplemental iron. |
| **Whole Wheat Bran Atta** | Table B (Cereals) | Trace | **840 mg / 100g** | Hexaphosphate rings of phytic acid chelate $\text{Fe}^{2+}$ into insoluble complexes. |
| **Nimbu Pani (Swap)** | Table F (Fruits) | Trace | Negligible | **Ascorbic acid (Vitamin C: 39 mg/100g)** reduces $\text{Fe}^{3+}$ to soluble $\text{Fe}^{2+}$, boosting uptake 3-fold! |
